Educational Note — Corpus-Derived Tokenization: Tokenization converts raw text into discrete integer IDs. In this educational pipeline, the vocabulary is genuinely derived from the training corpus without hardcoding. When a word was not observed in whole form (such as cats or learned), the greedy longest-match algorithm decomposes it using corpus-learned stems (cat, learn) and continuation subwords (##s, ##ed), where continuation tokens have their own distinct IDs. Unsegmentable words (such as zebra) fall back to a single [UNK] token (ID 0). Punctuation marks are preserved as separate tokens.

Embeddings & Mean Pooling

Tokens are mapped to 32-dimensional vectors. A sentence embedding is formed via mean pooling (the component-wise average of all token vectors). In an actual LLM, embeddings are learned parameters; here they are deterministically generated from token character hashes for educational clarity.

Cosine Similarity

Measures the cosine of the angle between two multi-dimensional vectors: cos(θ) = (A · B) / (||A|| ||B||). A score of +1.0 denotes identical direction regardless of length. Sentences sharing identical tokens exhibit higher simulated similarity.

Approximate PCA (Power Iteration)

Reduces 32-dimensional vectors to 3 visual dimensions by approximating the orthogonal axes of greatest variance. We calculate the mean-centered covariance matrix and estimate the top eigenvectors using iterative power iteration and Hotelling's deflation.
